# **SweepSafe \- Pitch Deck**

---

## **1\. Problem**

Los Angeles drivers rack up millions in street cleaning fines every year due to confusing signage and inconsistent schedules. Visitors and locals alike get caught off guard.

---

## **2\. Solution**

SweepSafe is a mobile-first web app that uses your phone’s location and LA's open data to alert you before your car gets ticketed. It's like "Find My Parking" meets Waze for street cleaning.

---

## **3\. How It Works**

* Detects when and where you park using GPS

* Matches your parked spot with LA’s street sweeping schedule

* Sends you smart alerts before your car is at risk

* Log in with Google, Facebook, or X.com

* View your parking history and customize alert preferences

---

## **4\. Why Now?**

* Open data initiatives by LA make real-time schedule matching possible

* Web push \+ SMS technologies allow instant alerts

* 90% of LA drivers have smartphones

---

## **5\. Market**

* 4.5M drivers in Greater LA

* \~500K street cleaning tickets issued annually

* $30M+ in fines per year \= high urgency and willingness to try solutions

---

## **6\. Competition**

* Apps like SpotAngels show general rules, but don’t detect actual parked state

* SweepSafe is fully automated with real-time risk alerts

---

## **7\. Business Model**

* Free for all users

* Optional upgrade: $1.99/mo for SMS alerts, longer parking history, and widget support

* Affiliate partnerships for parking/ticket payment services

---

## **8\. Go-To-Market**

* Launch web app for LA only

* Partner with local influencers \+ neighborhood groups

* Use parking ticket envelopes for QR code promotion

* Launch on Product Hunt \+ local tech forums
