## **SweepSafe \- Design Guidelines**

### **Visual Style**

* **Tone:** Trustworthy, lightweight, local-first

* **Color Palette:**

  * Sky Blue (alerts \+ highlights)

  * Asphalt Grey (backgrounds)

  * Caution Yellow (warnings)

  * White and soft neutrals for contrast

* **Fonts:**

  * San Francisco (native iOS)

  * Clear, medium-weight headlines

  * Small body text optimized for quick reading

* **Icons:**

  * Apple SF Symbols where possible

  * Map pins, car icons, street sweepers

### **Page Layout Principles**

* **Map First:** Main screen centers around a map

* **Card Overlays:** Countdown and sweep details in bottom drawer or overlay card

* **Sticky UI:** Parked info stays visible until user moves again

* **Minimalist Settings:** Simple toggles and options, grouped clearly

### **Accessibility and Mobile-First**

* High contrast UI with large tap targets

* Full VoiceOver support

* Dynamic Type for font scaling

* Dark mode supported by default

* Haptics for feedback on key actions (e.g., parking detected)

### **Brand Tone/Voice**

* Friendly but direct

* Avoid clutter or excess jargon

* Local-first: occasional LA-style flavor in alerts (e.g., "Heads up — your side’s up next")

* Transparent about what the app can/can’t guarantee
